Ewald Sphere Explorer

The circle is the 2D cross-section of the Ewald sphere: every reciprocal lattice point it touches satisfies the von Laue condition and diffracts. Rotate the crystal or change the electron wavelength and watch reflections sweep on and off the circle.

A reflection (h, k) diffracts when the momentum transfer lands exactly on a reciprocal lattice point, q = Ghk — the von Laue condition. Off that condition by a small amount, the mismatch is the excitation error from Lecture 5: sg = |Ghk − C| − k where C is the Ewald circle's centre. A thicker or more perfect crystal narrows the tolerance on sg — its reciprocal lattice "spots" sharpen into points; a thin or imperfect one broadens them into the relrods this slider approximates.
