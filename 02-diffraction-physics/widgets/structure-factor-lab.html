Hover the pattern to read off intensity at any (h,k).
Each unit cell has a basis of atoms at fractional positions (uj, vj) with scattering strength fj. The amplitude diffracted into order (h, k) is the structure factor: S(h,k) = Σj fj · e−2πi(h·uj + k·vj) and the measured intensity is I(h,k) ∝ |S(h,k)|² · [sharpening from N₁ × N₂ finite repeats]. When the basis atoms interfere destructively — S(h,k) = 0 — that reflection is systematically absent, no matter how strong each individual atom scatters.
